# top-social — community and social platforms.
#
# The crawler honours each site's robots.txt, so the big walled gardens
# (Instagram, TikTok, X) contribute only what they choose to expose to
# crawlers — usually little. The open and federated platforms below are the
# ones that actually reward a crawl, and their sitemaps (now auto-discovered)
# do most of the work of reaching individual profiles and posts.
